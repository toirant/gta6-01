SOLMAR CITY
Sun, salt air, and trouble — an open-world crime story
Mara Voss came to Solmar with $250, a borrowed pistol and a name to make. The marina crews, the couriers and the Solmar PD all want a piece of her. Take the jobs. Keep the money. Don't get caught.
Drive with WASD, steer with the mouse camera. Press E near a car to get in — parked cars are free, traffic cars are a crime. The police notice. Wanted stars rise when you steal, fight or shoot, and fall when you lay low. Finish the three jobs on the map to make your name. F punches, mouse click fires the pistol. M starts the next mission. Esc pauses.
An original game. Solmar City, Mara Voss and every street, car and song are invented here — nothing from any commercial game.
